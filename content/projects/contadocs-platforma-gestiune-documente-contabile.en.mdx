## Context

ContaDocs was my own SaaS product, built between October 2021 and January 2024 (over 900 commits). **The project didn't continue:** the market showed too little interest, and similar tools for accountants already existed. The platform is no longer online; this page stays as a case study.

## The problem

Every month, an accountant's clients have to send invoices, receipts and bank statements, and the accountant has to collect them and sort them by company and month. The idea was a deliberately minimal platform: as few actions as possible for the accountant, no more chasing documents for the client, and no documents passing through someone else's servers.

## What I built

- **Two separate apps**, one for the accountant and one for their clients.
- **The accountant account**, created with a Google account. The app asks for access only to the Google Drive files it creates, not to the whole Drive.
- **Company management:** the accountant adds their clients' companies, with the phone number of each company's representative, and can upload monthly reports for them.
- **An automatic folder structure** in the accountant's Drive: `ContaDocs / Company / Year / Month`, and inside each month `Invoices` (`Clients`, `Suppliers`), `Bank statements` and `Reports`. If a folder is deleted by mistake, the platform recreates it the next time it's used.
- **Passwordless client accounts:** clients sign in with the phone number the accountant added and a one-time code sent by SMS (Firebase). No extra password to remember for an app used once a month.
- **Several companies per client:** a representative picks the company, then browses down to the destination folder, e.g. `2023 › December › Invoices › Suppliers`.
- **Document upload** into `Clients`, `Suppliers` and `Bank statements`: JPG, PNG, PDF, DOC and DOCX. On a phone, clients can photograph receipts and invoices page by page, and the photos are merged into one PDF before upload.

## Under the hood

The app ran on Next.js, with NextAuth for the accountant's Google account and Firebase for the SMS codes, and Prisma for accounts, companies and sign-in sessions. Every file operation (the folder structure, uploads, downloads and deletes) went through the Google Drive API, with the accountant's tokens refreshed on the server. ContaDocs never kept a copy of any document, not even temporarily: files went straight into the accountant's Drive, to remove any privacy concerns. It ran in a Docker container.

The one limit I couldn't work around: for free Google accounts, an app can't lock the folders it creates, so I went with recreating the structure automatically.

## Status

The project is discontinued. Development took a long time and visibility among accountants stayed low, so it never got real feedback from the market. With little interest and similar solutions already available, I didn't build the planned notifications, monetization and other features, and shut the platform down.

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, NextAuth, Google Drive API, Firebase Authentication (SMS), Prisma, Docker.
