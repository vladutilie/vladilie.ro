## Context

Slam Basketball Academy is a youth basketball club. Since 2024 I've built and maintained the WordPress plugin that runs its sign-up process end to end, from the public form a parent fills in to the screens the club works from every day.

## The problem

Almost everyone in the system is a minor. Registering a child means collecting a parent's contact details and a medical certificate, then tracking monthly fees and reminding families when one is due. That data is sensitive, so the challenge wasn't just building forms — it was keeping as little as possible, storing what's needed safely and making sure nobody can probe the system for who is registered.

## What I built

- **A registration form** for the child and the parent, with the required consents. Each registration stays unconfirmed until the parent enters a 6-digit code sent **by SMS, with an automatic fallback to email**. A confirmation email follows through Brevo.
- **Medical certificates**, uploaded either as a document or replaced by the self-declaration the parent accepts, with the exact accepted text stored.
- **A parent portal** reached through a one-time magic link, where parents confirm pending registrations and upload certificates — no account or password needed.
- **Fee tracking** per child, with history, CSV export and monthly email and SMS reminders sent through WP-CLI, plus an unsubscribe page.
- **Admin screens and a dashboard widget** drawn as inline SVG: confirmed and pending registrations, recent activity, fees this month and certificate status, with links to filtered lists.

## Under the hood

Privacy shaped most of the technical decisions:

- **Certificates never touch the public web.** They are validated in memory and pushed to a private Cloudflare R2 bucket through a small hand-written S3 client signed with SigV4. Admins view them through presigned links that expire after five minutes.
- **Files are checked by content, not by name.** PDFs with JavaScript, embedded files or auto-actions are rejected; images are re-encoded, which strips EXIF data, including GPS.
- **Only hashes are stored** for verification codes and magic-link tokens, with short lifetimes and counted attempts.
- **No enumeration**: the portal answers the same way whether or not an address is registered, sits behind Cloudflare Turnstile and is rate-limited per email and per IP.
- CSV exports escape formula injection, and deleting a child removes every stored copy of their certificate.

The code is namespaced and PSR-4, follows WordPress Coding Standards, passes PHPStan level 5 and has around 180 PHPUnit tests. GitHub Actions runs the checks and deploys to staging or production.

## Result

The club runs its whole registration season from WordPress: parents sign up and confirm from their phones, certificates are stored privately and fees are tracked in one place, with reminders going out automatically.

## Stack

WordPress, PHP, Cloudflare R2 and Turnstile, Brevo, SMS gateway, WP-CLI, Tailwind CSS 4, PHPStan, PHPUnit, GitHub Actions.
